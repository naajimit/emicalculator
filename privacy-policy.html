Privacy Policy

Welcome to Calculator Of EMI. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy clearly explains how our website operates and how user information is handled.

No Data Collection

Calculator Of EMI does not collect, store, track, or share any personal information from its users. We do not require registration, login, or submission of any personal details to use our EMI calculator tool.

All loan details entered into the calculator — including loan amount, interest rate, and tenure — are processed directly within your own browser. These values are used only to perform the EMI calculation in real time and are not transmitted, saved, or stored on any server.

No Cookies

Calculator Of EMI does not use cookies to collect user information. We do not track user activity, browsing behavior, or financial inputs through cookies or similar technologies.

No Analytics or Tracking

We do not use analytics tools, tracking scripts, or third-party monitoring services that collect identifiable user data. Your visit to Calculator Of EMI remains completely private from our side.

Data Security

Since Calculator Of EMI does not collect or store user information, there is no personal or financial data retained by us. The calculations are performed locally within your browser environment, ensuring that your inputs remain under your control.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy in the future to reflect any changes in website functionality. Any updates will be published on this page.
